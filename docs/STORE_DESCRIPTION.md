# Chrome Web Store Description: Bookmark Sync

## 🔖 Bookmark Sync: Private, Versioned & Fast
**Sync your bookmarks to your own private GitHub repository. No third-party servers, no data collection, just your bookmarks under your control.**

Bookmark Sync is a powerful browser extension for users who value privacy and control over their data. It transforms your private GitHub repository into a personal, encrypted-at-rest bookmark synchronization engine.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

*   **🔒 Full Privacy & Control** — Your bookmarks never touch our servers. They are synced directly between your browser and your private GitHub repository via the GitHub API.
*   **💻 Multi-Device Support** — Set up unique profile names (e.g., "Work Laptop", "Home iMac"). Each device gets its own folder, allowing you to browse and search across all your devices without overwriting data.
*   **🕰️ Infinite Version History** — Every sync that detects a change saves a historical snapshot. Accidentally deleted a folder? Just roll back to a previous version or browse your bookmarks as they were last week.
*   **🔍 Lightning-Fast Search** — Powerful fuzzy search across titles, URLs, and folder paths. Results are ranked by relevance, helping you find that one tab from three months ago in milliseconds.
*   **📂 True Folder Hierarchy** — Unlike other sync tools, Bookmark Sync preserves your entire nested folder structure exactly as it is in Chrome.
*   **📥 Universal Export** — Download your bookmarks at any time as a standard Netscape HTML file, compatible with any major browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
*   **🌓 Modern UI** — Beautifully designed interface with smooth transitions and full support for both Dark and Light modes.

---

### 🛡️ Why Choose Bookmark Sync?

Most synchronization tools store your data on their own proprietary servers. Bookmark Sync gives you the power of the cloud without the privacy risk. Since you own the repository, you own the data.

---

### 🛠️ Quick Start Guide

1.  **Create a private GitHub repository** (e.g., `my-bookmarks`).
2.  **Generate a Personal Access Token (PAT)** on GitHub with `repo` scope (or fine-grained access to your bookmarks repo).
3.  **Install the extension** and open the **Settings** (⚙️).
4.  **Connect your account** by entering your username, repo name, and token.
5.  **Choose a Profile Name** and set your sync frequency.
6.  **Click Save** — and your bookmarks are instantly safe and synced!
